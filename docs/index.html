WHY THIS EXISTS

Calls can arrive
at busy times.

A call can come in at an inconvenient time. A recovery workflow gives that caller another way to tell you what they need.

  • Technicians are in the middle of a job
  • Staff are already helping someone on another call
  • Several customers call at once
  • The office is closed or an urgent call arrives after hours

If these situations happen in your business, there may be a practical way to keep the conversation moving.
