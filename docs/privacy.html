Privacy policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how Made Forever handles information connected with this informational website and inquiries about our services.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, company, contact details, and the content of your inquiry. Please do not send sensitive personal, financial, health, or customer information.

Website visits

This website has no contact form, account registration, advertising pixels, or analytics tools added by Made Forever. We do not set cookies or use browser storage on this site. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to respond, understand your business needs, discuss a possible service setup, and maintain related business records. Sending an inquiry does not enroll you in a marketing text-message program.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell inquiry information. Information may be processed by providers we use for website hosting, business email, and telephone communications, as needed to handle your inquiry. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information as needed for the conversation, related business purposes, and applicable obligations. We use reasonable care to protect it, but no electronic communication or storage method can be guaranteed secure.

Your choices

You can ask about information you have shared, request correction or deletion, or ask us to stop contacting you by emailing team@madeforever.live. We will review requests subject to applicable obligations and any need to retain business records.

Scope and updates

This site is intended for business audiences, not children. This policy covers the public website and direct inquiries; any customer-specific recovery service may need separate terms and privacy arrangements. We may update this policy by posting a revised version here.
