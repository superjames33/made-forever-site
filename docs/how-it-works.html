THE MISSED-CALL RECOVERY SYSTEM

A clear path from
unanswered to follow-up.

Made Forever helps service businesses create a practical next step when a call goes unanswered. Here’s how the pieces fit together.

Your phone.
Your team.
A recovery path.

Missed-call automation connects an unanswered call to a response and an organized opportunity for your team.

It can support after-hours call follow-up and home service call automation, with the workflow tailored to your operation.

Talk through your setup ↗
  1. 01

    Incoming call

    A customer calls your business number. Your existing call-handling process comes first.

  2. 02

    Unanswered-call event

    Where your phone system supports it, an unanswered call provides the event that starts the recovery workflow. We confirm how this works with your provider.

  3. 03

    Recovery response

    A configured response, such as a missed-call text, gives the caller a way to continue the conversation. Text delivery depends on a text-capable number and provider support.

  4. 04

    Customer provides a basic reason for calling

    The response can collect the service needed, basic contact details, and information useful for a callback. It should not request sensitive information.

  5. 05

    Lead gets organized

    The caller’s details and request are organized and routed to the person or team responsible for follow-up.

  6. 06

    Your team follows up

    Your team calls back or continues the agreed follow-up workflow. Timing, routing, and responsibilities are defined during setup.

START WITH YOUR EXISTING SETUP

No one-size-fits-all
phone promise.

We work around your existing phone setup whenever practical instead of forcing you to replace it.

Implementation depends on your current phone provider, available unanswered-call events, call routing, and messaging support. We review those details before recommending changes.

We also agree on the response, what information to capture, who receives each opportunity, and how follow-up should work. Where texting is used, setup includes applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

A recovery response is not a confirmed booking, an emergency dispatch service, or a guarantee of revenue. Your team remains responsible for responding to customer needs.
